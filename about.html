About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a small family kitchen with a simple goal: to bring neighbors together around warm, handmade food. Today we are a neighborhood gathering place where regulars are greeted by name and every loaf is shaped by hand.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We buy local whenever we can. Our flour comes from regional mills, our eggs and dairy from nearby farms, and our fruit from seasonal growers. Good ingredients make honest baking, and they keep our community's farmers working.
